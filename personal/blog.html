A Space of One's Own

October 12, 2026

A custom website with (almost) no third-party dependencies and tech companies interferring - that is what freedom in the ages of the wild world web must have felt like. You remain in full control over every aspect of design and functionality - and can create a space that is truly your own. I am grateful for the opportunity to share my thoughts and reflections in this space, and I hope you enjoy exploring it as much as I have enjoyed creating it. The best part? Creativity and self-expression are not limited by the constraints of social media platforms or the algorithms that govern them. Which is excitingly old-fashioned; the world of AI has actually opened up the good ol wild web. Free at last, free at last. (Thanks GitHub + github Pages)
