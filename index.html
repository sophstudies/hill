Research Interests

  • LLMs and Psychology:
    • LLM/VLM performance in reading and interpreting social and behavioral cues - compared to human performance
    • Neuropsychological Tasks for AI systems: exploring how artificial intelligence systems process and represent knowledge
    • How human are LLMs? What can semantic networks and psycholinguistic clusters tell us about the human cognition and experience?
    • How much 'humanity' and human-like behavior do people attribute to AI systems? Do contemporary AI companions make the Turing Test irrelevant?
  • Ethics of AI applications:
    • Human-AI Interaction - how to ensure responsible and reasonable use of AI in psychological research and practice
    • Human-AI Communication - how does AI application usage impact decision-making, language production, interpersonal relationships?
    • User Privacy and Security in AI Applications - how can we protect sensitive personal information when relying on commercial AI systems?
    • Artificial Suffering and Consciousness - problems of Synthetic Phenomenology and Embodied Cognition
    • Embodied Cognition & phenomenology in Humans (Husserl, Merleau-Ponty) vs. embodied cognition & Extended Mind in AI Systems (Clark&Chalmers)
  • Linguistics and semiotics in psychology:
    • Linguistic Inquiry and Word Count (LIWC, Pennebaker) in personality psychology
    • Narrative Construction Theory
    • Memory and knowledge representation: how can somatic, semantic, symbolic, dissociative and affective memory inform present neurobiological and socio-psychological frameworks on human memory?
  • Beyond STEM: Survivor Epistemology
    • How can survivor epistemology inform Critical Theory and Empirical Research?
    • Social Motivated Blindness
    • Sexism in personality perception concerning the Dark Triad/Tetrad (Narcissism, Machiavellianism, Psychopathy, Sadism)
    • Mechanisms of Institutional Abuse, The perfect victim narrative, and the role of sexism in abuse disclosure
    • Legal proceedings involving testimony; triage of social, familial and economic motifs
    • Advantages and pitfalls of social media in survivor advocacy
